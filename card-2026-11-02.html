Horse

GOOD
Has run close to (or beaten) a typical winning time at this course & distance in at least half of 3+ runs there. Row is tinted green.
★ Star
Quickest typical (median) time in this race at this course & distance. Needs 2+ runs here, and at least two horses to compare.
🐎 Breeding
How often the sire's runners win: his flat record in a flat race, his jumps record over hurdles, fences and in bumpers. green = well above the average sire, amber = well below. Hover (or tap) for the sire's Group wins and the dam's other foals. Our tests show breeding is already in the price.
J / T
Jockey and trainer. Tap + beside a horse to show them, or + J/T at the top to show them all. Hover (or tap) for their winners in the last 14 days and the owner. 🔥 = 25%+ winners from 5+ runs in that time — note our tests show the market already prices in-form yards.
Back
Best price to back it on the Betfair Exchange when this page was made.
Lay
Best price to lay it (bet against it) at the same time.
Risk
What a €1 lay stands to lose if the horse wins (lay price − 1). Orange at 10+.
Mark
Official handicap rating today. In brackets: how much it has gone up (+) or down (−) since it last won or finished 2nd.
Class
Class of its last race. Arrow = today vs that: ↑ stepping up (tougher), ↓ dropping down, = same. Class 1 is the best, 7 the lowest. Greyed if that race was a while ago.
Figure
Our speed figure: the average of its last 3 runs. Higher = ran faster.
Early
Where it usually is early in a race: 0 = leading, 1 = last.
Pace
Early position rank in this field: #1 = most likely to be at the back, the highest number = most likely to lead.
Closes
How much faster (+) or slower (−) than the field it usually runs the last 2 furlongs.
Runs
Past runs behind these numbers. In brackets: how often it ran close to a typical winning time.
Fastest
Its quickest time at today's course & distance.
Median
Its typical (middle) time there.
Speed map
Under each race: the horse's average speed in each part of its past races compared with the field it was running in. Green = faster than the field, red = slower, yellow = about level.
