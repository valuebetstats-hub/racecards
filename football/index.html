How to read the card

Betfair: best back and lay on the Betfair Exchange when the card was made (time at the top). Where Betfair has no market yet, the bookmaker average is shown instead.

Our model v market: the coloured bar is our model's chance of a home win, draw and away win. It rates every team on goals scored and conceded, weighted to recent games and the strength of its division, rebuilt after every match since 2012. No prices go into it. Underneath is the market's chance from the prices, with the bookmaker margin removed. Our tests have found the market hard to beat: when the two disagree, the market has been right more often.

Goals, corners, cards: our model's chance of over 2.5 goals, both teams scoring, over 9.5 corners and over 3.5 cards. The cards figure allows for the referee's career cards per game. xG is the model's expected goals for each side.

Form and records: last five league results (most recent on the right). Home record = home wins this season and the team's rank on our home-win board; likewise away wins and draws.
