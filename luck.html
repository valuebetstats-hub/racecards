How it works

A price is a forecast. At decimal odds of 5.0 the market says a horse wins 1 time in 5, and on Betfair that forecast is close to spot on. So we can ask: if your bets had no edge at all, how would they have done?

The checker replays your exact bets thousands of times, letting each horse win with the chance its price gave it, and records the profit each time. That's what pure luck looks like at your odds. Then it counts how often luck did as well as you.

The maths

Chance from the price: p = 1 ÷ odds. Expected winners E = sum of p. Luck spread SD = √(sum of p × (1 − p)). The 95% luck range of winners is E ± 1.96 × SD. A/E = your winners ÷ E. Back profit per winner = (odds − 1) × stake × (1 − commission); a loser costs the stake. Break-even strike rate at one price = 1 ÷ (1 + (odds − 1) × (1 − commission)). Bookmaker prices carry a margin, so 1 ÷ odds slightly overstates the true chance and the check is a little kind to you. Betfair SP is the fairest test.
